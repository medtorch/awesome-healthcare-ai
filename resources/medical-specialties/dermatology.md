# Dermatology :heavy_exclamation_mark:

Dermatologists are physicians who treat adult and pediatric patients with disorders of the skin, hair, nails, and adjacent mucous membranes. They diagnose everything from skin cancer, tumors, inflammatory diseases of the skin, and infectious diseases. They also perform skin biopsies and dermatological surgical procedures.

Subspecialties within the dermatology field include the following:

* Dermatopathology
* * Pediatric dermatology
  * * Procedural dermatology
    * * Cosmetic dermatology
      * * Mohs surgery

                - ### Research Papers
                - - [Dermatologist-level classification of skin cancer with deep neural networks](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature21056) - Esteva et al. (2017). Landmark paper demonstrating CNN-based skin cancer classification matching dermatologist performance across 129,450 clinical images. Published in Nature.
                  - - [Skin Lesion Analysis Toward Melanoma Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03368) - Codella et al. (2019). Comprehensive benchmark and challenge paper from the ISIC archive, establishing standardized evaluation for dermoscopic image analysis.
                    - - [A deep learning system for differential diagnosis of skin diseases](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-020-0842-3) - Liu et al. (2020). Google Health study covering 26 skin conditions across diverse populations. Published in Nature Medicine.
                      - - [Classification of Skin Lesions Using Transfer Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09042) - Harangi (2018). Demonstrated effectiveness of combining multiple pretrained CNNs (AlexNet, VGGNet, GoogLeNet) for skin lesion classification using ISIC data.
                        - - [Artificial Intelligence in Dermatology: A Systematic Review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jdv.16032) - Brinker et al. (2019). Systematic review of AI applications across dermatology covering diagnosis, teledermatology, and treatment planning. Published in JEADV.
                         
                          - ### Open-source
                          - - [ISIC Archive Tools](https://github.com/ImageMarkup/isic-archive) - Official tools for interacting with the International Skin Imaging Collaboration archive, the largest public repository of dermoscopic images.
                            - - [Skin Lesion Analyzer](https://github.com/abhishekdbihani/Skin-Lesion-Analyzer) - Deep learning application for classifying skin lesions using transfer learning on dermoscopic images.
                              - - [DermNet](https://github.com/mattgroh/fitzpatrick17k) - Fitzpatrick17k dataset tools for evaluating dermatology AI across skin tones, addressing bias in skin lesion classification.
                                - - [OpenDerm](https://github.com/OpenDerm) - Open-source dermatology image analysis framework supporting multiple classification tasks.
                                 
                                  - ### Datasets
                                  - - [ISIC 2019 Challenge Dataset](https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/) - 25,331 dermoscopic images across 8 diagnostic categories. The standard benchmark for skin lesion classification research.
                                    - - [HAM10000](https://dataverse.harvard.edu/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.7910/DVN/DBW86T) - 10,015 dermatoscopic images of pigmented skin lesions across 7 clinically significant categories. Published in Nature Scientific Data.
                                      - - [Fitzpatrick17k](https://github.com/mattgroh/fitzpatrick17k) - 16,577 clinical images annotated with Fitzpatrick skin type labels (I-VI) and disease labels, designed for evaluating fairness across skin tones.
                                        - - [PH2 Dataset](https://www.fc.up.pt/addi/ph2%20database.html) - 200 dermoscopic images with expert annotations for melanoma, atypical nevi, and common nevi. Includes lesion segmentation masks.
                                          - - [DermNet NZ](https://dermnetnz.org/) - Comprehensive dermatology resource with over 20,000 clinical images organized by condition, widely used as a reference in dermatology AI research.
                                           
                                            - ### Startups
                                            - - [MetaOptima](https://www.metaoptima.com/) - AI-powered dermatology platform for clinical image management, lesion tracking, and diagnostic support.
                                              - - [SkinVision](https://www.skinvision.com/) - Smartphone-based skin cancer risk assessment using AI analysis of skin lesion photos.
                                                - - [3Derm](https://www.3derm.com/) - AI-powered teledermatology platform providing instant skin condition assessments for healthcare systems.
